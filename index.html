Good to know

A few things you may be wondering.

The answers below are starter copy. Confirm your actual service policies and update these before launch.

Do you offer pickup and delivery?

Pickup and delivery are intended as part of the service. Availability, fees and time windows should be confirmed for your PIN code before accepting a request.

How do I know if my area is covered?

Submit your PIN code in the pickup form. The BOL team should confirm whether service is available in your locality before finalizing the booking.

How long will my laundry take?

Turnaround depends on the service, garment type and local capacity. BOL should confirm the expected delivery date when the pickup is accepted.

How much does it cost?

Pricing is being finalized in this preview. Ask for the current rate card, any minimum order, pickup/delivery fee and applicable taxes before confirming.

Can I request special garment care?

Yes, you can describe delicate items or special requirements in the notes field. The team should confirm whether the item can be handled and quote any additional charges.

When is my pickup confirmed?

Your request is not confirmed until the BOL team contacts you and agrees on availability, price and the pickup time.
